# Annotation guide

## Unit of annotation

Annotate the comment in the context of the video's title and description. Do
not infer facts about the author or commenter. Usernames are not annotation
features.

## Labels

### Sentiment

- **Positive** — the comment expresses approval, satisfaction or praise about the video or its content.
- **Neutral** — factual, unclear, purely inquisitive, or without a clear evaluative stance.
- **Negative** — criticism, dissatisfaction or rejection directed at the video or its content.

### Toxicity

Mark true only for insulting, demeaning, threatening or aggressively hostile
language. Disagreement and ordinary criticism are not automatically toxic.

### Relevance

Mark true when the comment evaluates, discusses or asks a substantive question
about the video. Attendance comments, self-promotion, generic channel greetings
and unrelated conversation are irrelevant.

## Ambiguity and adjudication

Annotators may mark a note but must still choose a label. Every gold-set item is
labelled independently by three people. Disagreements are resolved only after
agreement statistics are calculated. The adjudicator records the final label
and a one-sentence rationale.

## Evidence annotation

For each video, choose up to three relevant comments that directly support the
overall reaction. Prefer coverage of distinct reasons over repeated wording.
